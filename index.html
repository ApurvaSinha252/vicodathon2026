Before you commit

Good questions, honest answers

Do I need to already know how to code?

No. Day 1 tasks are built for someone who's only done classroom basics. The difficulty ramps up gradually across the 60 days.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak resets, but nothing is deleted. Your full history stays visible on your profile — most students who restart finish stronger the second time.

What counts as "proof of work"?

A GitHub commit showing what you built, plus a short LinkedIn post about it. Both together — code without visibility doesn't build your name.

Is it really free?

Yes. ABTalks is free for students, always. You just need a GitHub account and a LinkedIn profile to submit proof.
